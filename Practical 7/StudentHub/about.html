About StudentHub

A clearer view of student life

We bring your everyday academic tools together, helping you spend less time searching and more time learning.

Our purpose

Everything in one student-friendly place

StudentHub is a lightweight portal created for CSPIT students. It brings assignments, grades, timetables, attendance, resources, notices, and campus events into one easy-to-navigate experience.

Whether you're checking a deadline on your phone or reviewing your weekly schedule on a laptop, the portal keeps the information you need close at hand.
